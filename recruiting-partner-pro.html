Recruiting Partner Pro is full embedded recruiting: a dedicated Metcalf Search recruiter onsite three days a week, every open role in your agreed scope, for a flat $10,950 a month. No per-hire fees. Month to month.

Who this is for.

Companies hiring constantly: a plant adding a shift, a branch opening, growth that needs ten or twenty hires this year and a recruiting function that does not exist yet. The alternative is hiring a recruiter, with the salary, tools, ramp and management that come with it.

What a month buys.

  • A dedicated recruiter onsite three full days a week. Embedded with your managers, on a set schedule, running every open role in your agreed scope.
  • All roles in your agreed scope. We agree on the roles covered at kickoff and plan the work with you.
  • Support through each stage of hiring. A sourcing plan, agreed updates, structured interviews, offer preparation, and check-ins during the first ninety days for each hire.
  • Talent Points included. These are credits toward approved recruiting expenses, including job advertising. See how Talent Points work.
  • MS Rosie hiring software for your whole company. Careers page, applicant tracking and hiring numbers you can put in front of the owner.
  • A recruiting function your company keeps. The process, the scorecards and the pipeline live in MS Rosie, not in our heads.
  • A single point of contact on our team. Your contact keeps you informed, coordinates the people working on your search, and helps you move the next decision forward.

Central Ohio onsite; other locations by arrangement.

What happens after you click.

  1. Today

    You activate. A confirmation lands in your inbox within minutes, and you book the kickoff on the next page.

  2. Target: within 48 hours

    Kickoff. A one-hour intake across every open role in your agreed scope, and the onsite schedule set. Sourcing starts the same day.

  3. Target: five business days

    Our starting target is the first vetted candidates within five business days of kickoff. We confirm priorities and timing for each role together.

  4. Week two

    Our target for first interviews, depending on each role and candidate availability. We schedule them, prep both sides and debrief after each one.

  5. Thirty to forty-five days

    A planning estimate for filling a role. Specialized searches may need forty-five to sixty days or longer. We discuss timing at kickoff, and new-hire check-ins begin when the hire starts.

The result.

Your managers have a recruiter working on every open role in your agreed scope and someone they can go to with questions. When the hiring rush settles down, that recruiter continues to support the team.

The math.

Twelve months of Recruiting Partner Pro is $131,400. Ten contingency hires at a $65,000 salary and a 25 percent fee cost $162,500. Twenty cost $325,000. A full-time in-house recruiter costs salary, payroll taxes, tools and job boards, plus time to learn your company. We aim to hold kickoff within 48 hours of activation. Pro runs month to month. Cancel with ten days’ notice. A larger offer does not increase our monthly fee, and there is no placement fee when someone starts. Our job is to keep the search moving and help you make the right hire.

Three questions.

Who is onsite?
A Metcalf Search recruiter dedicated to your company, three full days a week, on a set schedule. You have a single point of contact on our team, with support from the people working on your search.
Are there any per-hire fees?
No. There is no placement fee when you hire someone. The monthly fee covers your plan’s agreed recruiting work. Optional extra onsite days and approved travel or other expenses are separate, as described in the partnership agreement.
What if hiring slows down?
Move down to Recruiting Partner Plus or Recruiting Partner when your hiring needs change. Tier changes take effect immediately. Upgrades have a prorated charge; downgrades have a credit toward future invoices, not a cash refund. Your careers page, pipeline and records stay in MS Rosie.

If a hire leaves during your partnership, see how replacement searches work.
