The story

He knows what’s
on your side
of the desk.

When you hire, you’re deciding who your team will depend on and where someone will spend their working day. Jay has made those decisions as both a recruiter and the person running the business.

01

An early start in business.

Growing up in rural Ohio, Jay started with a paper route and mowing lawns at nine. As a teenager, he bought auctioned storage units and sold what was inside.

02

Recruiting, then running the business.

At IntelliCap Recruiting (2004–2009), Jay recruited, trained recruiters, and built a candidate-management training system. From December 2012 to January 2016, he was general manager of the staffing business Barracuda International.

With full responsibility for its profit and loss, he grew revenue from under fifty thousand dollars to more than ten million in three years. He was responsible for the people, the work, and the numbers.

03

Metcalf Search today.

Since December 2024, Jay has focused on giving small businesses access to the recruiting tools and support they need.

Owners can work through the free STACK guides, manage hiring in MS Rosie, or bring in Metcalf Search to run searches with them.

The recruiter brain never really switches off.

A new neighbor mentioned she was looking for work as a baker. Jay knew a nearby business that might need her and suggested she apply. She got an interview, then the job. No search assignment. Just noticing who might need whom.

Miami University graduate · Recruiting, staffing, business operations and sales · See Jay’s professional background on LinkedIn ↗

Jay’s perspective

The person you hire
will be right there.
Ten feet away.

I want owners to have a real choice between good people. I want candidates to understand the job they are walking into. And I want the process to help both sides make a decision they can feel good about.

Technology can make the work easier. It can organize the details and keep things moving. But we still have to talk to people. That is where we find out what matters.

-Jay

The short bio.

For introductions, interviews and event hosts.

For interviews and speaking inquiries, email Metcalf Search.

Jay Metcalf is the founder and managing partner of Metcalf Search, a fractional recruiting firm based in Columbus, Ohio. His twenty-seven years in recruiting and staffing include more than $100 million in salary placed and hands-on responsibility for growing a staffing business. He created the STACK Method and built MS Rosie, hiring software for small businesses at apply.ms. A Miami University graduate, Jay is writing a practical book on small-business hiring. His work centers on a simple belief: hiring is personal, and the process should reflect that.
