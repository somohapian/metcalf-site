THE COMPARISON

Eleven ways to get hiring done.

Compare the work covered, the costs, and the help your team needs.

Start with who will do the work. Will someone on your team run the search, will you hire a recruiter, or will you bring in outside help? Then compare the actual responsibilities, capacity and total cost. The eleven options below are a checklist for that conversation, not a ranking of providers.

Three starting points

The main difference is who takes on the daily recruiting work. Here is our starting point for deciding which model to investigate. Specific services and responsibilities still need to be agreed.

Examples behind this overview: Indeed's free and paid posting options, BLS descriptions of recruiter duties, and Robert Half's recruiting process and placement-fee reporting. These illustrate the models, rather than terms promised by every provider.

Start with the decision in front of you

Doing it yourself? Name the person who will review applicants, contact candidates and keep interviews moving. Put that time on the calendar before buying another tool.

Considering an agency? Define the role and ask what happens between the first introduction and the start date. Confirm the fee, payment trigger and any replacement terms in writing.

Considering an internal recruiter or ongoing outside help? List the roles you expect to open, the work between searches and who will manage priorities. Compare an employee, a contract recruiter, a fractional recruiter and recruitment process outsourcing (RPO) against that same list.

Compare the same work

Write down one current role and the steps you need help with. Ask each provider to price that work, name the person responsible and explain what your team still needs to do. Include the cost of tools and your own time. Compare the total for the same expected hiring workload.

Look past the start date

Ask who stays in touch after the offer, who helps if the new hire struggles and what happens if they leave. A replacement promise and support for keeping a hire are different things. Get the details of each.
