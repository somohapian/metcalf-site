Who this is for.

Owners and HR managers with hiring that does not stop: two or three seats open most months, several managers to coordinate, and nobody with the hours to run it properly. Companies of roughly ten to two hundred people, in any industry.

What a month buys.

  • A senior recruiter is your single point of contact. Two to three roles at once, backed by the Metcalf Search team.
  • One onsite hour every week. Hiring manager time, interview debriefs and the decisions that stall over email. The same hour every week, so it actually happens.
  • The full STACK Method on every search. A sourcing plan, a tempo commitment, a structured assessment, an offer sequence and a ninety-day keep plan, per role.
  • Ad spend for every open role, included. Each job runs where candidates actually look, on our budget, not yours.

Three questions.

What counts as a concurrent role?
Any seat we are actively sourcing for. Recruiting Partner Plus runs up to three at once; when one fills, the next opens. More than three open most months is Recruiting Partner Pro territory.
Are there any per-hire fees?
No. The monthly subscription is the full cost. A filled role costs the same as an open one, and a bigger offer does not raise our fee.
What does the onsite hour cover?
One scheduled hour a week with your hiring managers: debriefs, decisions, offer strategy and whatever has stalled over email. It is the same hour every week so it actually happens.
Billing terms.

Recruiting Partner Plus is $5,950 a month, billed monthly in advance, month to month. Move up or down a tier at any monthly cycle; rates are grandfathered. Cancel with thirty days notice by emailing info@metcalfsearch.com. The partnership agreement, signed at kickoff, governs the partnership.
