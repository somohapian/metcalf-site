FOR CANDIDATES

A clear conversation about your next move.

Learn about the job, ask your questions, and know what happens next. We'll help you decide whether the opportunity fits what you want.

Metcalf Search recruits for businesses looking to hire. We discuss the work, pay, and expectations with you before an introduction, keep you informed through the process, and share the hiring company’s decision. There is no fee to candidates.

You should know where you stand

A job change is a decision for you as well as the employer. We want to understand the work you are looking for, the experience you bring, and the details that matter to you. Ask about the role, the team, the schedule, or anything else you need to make that decision.

You will have a recruiter to contact as the search moves forward. If you are no longer interested or your plans change, let us know. We will do the same when we have an update from the hiring company.

What working with Metcalf looks like as a candidate

If we have reached out to you or you have applied for one of our clients' roles, here is what to expect.

A conversation about the role. The first call is fifteen to thirty minutes. We discuss your experience, what you want from your next job, pay expectations, and practical details such as schedule and commute. There is time for your questions too.

An introduction with context. With your permission, we share your resume and a write-up explaining your relevant experience, strengths, availability, and interest in the role. That helps the hiring manager prepare for a useful conversation.

An outcome either way. When the client makes a decision, you hear about it. If you advance, you hear about it within a day or two. If you do not advance, you hear about it within the same window, with whatever feedback the client is willing to share.

Future opportunities. If this role does not work out, we can stay in contact with your permission. Let us know what you would like us to keep in mind for another search.

How we support your search

We can help you prepare for the specific roles we are recruiting for.

We explain the salary range and timeline, help you prepare for the interview, and point out relevant experience a hiring manager may miss on your resume. We also keep you informed when the employer makes a decision.

We do not charge candidates or send your information to a company without your permission. Our support focuses on the roles we are filling, rather than general career coaching or a complete resume rewrite.

Stay in the network

We read every entry and will reach out personally when a role fits your background. Tell us where you would like to work and whether you are open to remote roles or relocation.
