Partnership agreement: Recruiting Partner, Plus and Pro

This agreement governs every Metcalf Search fractional recruiting partnership. Activating a tier on its page accepts it.

This Agreement ("Agreement") is made between Metcalf Search, LLC, an Ohio limited liability company ("Metcalf"), and the entity or individual subscribing to Metcalf's services ("Client"). The "Effective Date" of this Agreement is the date of the Client's initial subscription payment.

Partnership commitment: this Agreement engages Metcalf as a strategic recruiting and retention partner. It reflects our shared commitment to ethical, effective, and human-centered recruiting that strives to solve the "who" for your operation.

1. Partnership tiers and scope of service

1.1. Core services. All Partnership Tiers include Metcalf's complete, end-to-end recruiting service: strategic planning (job description and scorecard development); full-cycle candidate sourcing, screening and pipeline management; interview coordination and hiring manager calibration; offer management, negotiation and closing support; professional management of all inbound applications; a retention toolkit to help keep new and existing employees; and MS Rosie hiring software (careers page and applicant tracking) with some job advertising funded through the Talent Points program described below.

1.2. Partnership service levels.

  • (a) Recruiting Partner. A strategic remote partnership. Includes a bi-weekly talent huddle (video), standard business day support by email and phone, occasional onsite support at Metcalf's discretion when agreed with Client, such as an onboarding session or interview day. This tier does not include scheduled monthly onsite hours. Customized retention toolkit. One (1) active role at a time.
  • (b) Recruiting Partner Plus. An embedded partnership. Includes a weekly talent huddle (video), standard business day support, and up to eight (8) hours of onsite time per month, scheduled with Client (for example, one full day or two half days). Enhanced, customized retention toolkit. Up to three (3) concurrent active roles.
  • (c) Recruiting Partner Pro. A fully integrated partnership. Includes a dedicated Metcalf recruiter onsite three (3) full days per week on a set schedule, priority support, and a weekly talent huddle. Enterprise, customized retention toolkit. All open roles within the scope agreed at kickoff.
  • (d) Onsite time. Included onsite time under Recruiting Partner Plus and Recruiting Partner Pro is available in Central Ohio. For other locations, onsite arrangements and any travel costs must be agreed in writing before subscription activation. Onsite time may be used for interview days, hiring manager sessions, debriefs, and strategy sessions with leadership. Unused onsite time does not roll over to a later month and has no cash value.
  • (e) Additional onsite days. Client may request onsite time beyond what the selected tier includes. Each additional day is billed at a flat $2,500, including standard travel within the continental United States.
  • (f) Expedited travel. Visits scheduled less than 14 days out, or international travel, may incur additional costs with Client approval. All visits are subject to availability.

1.3. Service fees. The monthly fee for the selected tier is defined in Client's active subscription order at checkout. By keeping an active subscription, Client locks in the rate in effect at the time of initial payment ("Grandfathered Rate").

2. Term and billing

2.1. Term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues month to month ("Term").

2.2. Cancellation. Client may end this Agreement for any reason with ten (10) days written notice. Termination is effective at the end of the monthly billing cycle in which the notice period is satisfied.

2.3. Billing. The first invoice, for one full monthly fee, is due on activation. It sets the "Billing Anniversary" and covers the first month of service. All later monthly fees are billed automatically on the Billing Anniversary each month.

2.4. Payment method. Client agrees to provide and maintain a valid, automated payment method (ACH transfer or card on file) for all recurring monthly fees.

2.5. Late payment. If any payment is more than seven (7) days past due, Metcalf may pause services until payment is received, and Client's payment obligations continue.

3. Partnership and tier management

3.1. Changing tiers. Client may change tiers at any time through the Metcalf Search subscription portal. (a) Upgrades take effect immediately, with a one-time prorated charge for the difference through the end of the current billing cycle; the new monthly fee applies from the next Billing Anniversary. (b) Downgrades take effect immediately, with a prorated credit for the unused part of the higher tier applied to future invoices. No cash refunds are issued for mid-cycle downgrades.

3.2. Continuous partnership guarantee. This model replaces traditional per-hire guarantees. If a candidate placed by Metcalf resigns or is terminated for performance while this Agreement is active, Metcalf reopens the search for that role as part of the ongoing service. The guarantee applies only to candidates placed during the active term and for roles supported under this Agreement.

4. The Talent Points program

Earned Talent Points are included in each paid Partnership Tier under the formula below. Additional bonus points may be awarded at Metcalf's discretion. Points are available only for approved recruiting expenses and have no cash value.

4.1. Earning points. Client earns one (1) Talent Point for every ten dollars ($10.00) of paid monthly fee. Metcalf may also award discretionary bonus points for promotions, referrals, or other milestones.

4.2. Redemption and value. One (1) Talent Point is redeemable for one dollar ($1.00) of spending power on approved recruiting expenses, which may include sponsored job posts, premium candidate assessments, background checks, and candidate signing bonuses. Other search-related expenses can be approved in writing.

4.3. No cash value. Talent Points have no cash value, are not transferable, and cannot be redeemed for cash or as a discount on fees. All accumulated Talent Points are forfeited when this Agreement ends for any reason.

5. Confidentiality and conflicts of interest

5.1. Confidentiality. Metcalf treats all non-public information relating to Client (including hiring plans, salary bands, and candidate data) as strictly confidential, to the standard of a trusted fiduciary. Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for two (2) years.

5.2. Conflicts of interest. Metcalf runs dedicated, separate searches for each client and never shares candidates between clients, except where a candidate applies independently or is otherwise publicly available. Metcalf will be fully transparent and seek Client's written consent if any potential conflict arises.

6. General terms

6.1. Fee disputes. Client agrees to resolve any fee dispute directly with Metcalf in good faith and not to initiate card chargebacks or bank payment disputes; doing so is a material breach of this Agreement.

6.2. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Ohio. The parties agree to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Franklin County, Ohio, for any dispute arising from this Agreement.

6.3. Scope of services. Metcalf acts as a strategic recruiting partner and advisor, not as legal counsel, an HR consultant, or a benefits administrator. All services, tools, and advice (including on compliance and retention) are best-practice frameworks from a talent acquisition perspective. Client is solely responsible for all final employment decisions, HR policies, and its own legal and regulatory compliance.

6.4. Effect of termination. (a) Grandfathered pricing holds only while the subscription stays continuously active; any cancellation, lapse, or non-payment ends it. (b) Reactivation after cancellation or lapse requires a new subscription at then-current pricing.

6.5. Limitation of liability. Except for gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of confidentiality, Metcalf's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed one (1) month of Client's current partnership fee.

6.6. Notices. Written notice under this Agreement may be given by email to info@metcalfsearch.com and to Client's email address on file.

6.7. Access to client systems. If Client gives Metcalf access to internal software, email, or data systems, that access is governed by a separate Data Security Addendum, which, once signed by both parties, is incorporated into this Agreement.

7. Acceptance and subscription

7.1. Acceptance. Client accepts this Agreement by making the initial subscription payment through the Metcalf Search payment portal.

7.2. Tier selection. Client's tier and monthly fee are confirmed by the subscription selected at checkout.

7.3. Binding effect. By keeping an active subscription, Client agrees to be bound by this Agreement, including its cancellation and pricing continuity terms.

MS Rosie

This section applies to MS Rosie Starter, the paid MS Rosie plan, and Rosie accounts included with a Metcalf Search recruiting partnership. MS Rosie is run by Metcalf Search, LLC and described at apply.ms. Where this section differs from the general site terms about Rosie, this section applies. Recruiting services are governed by the partnership agreement above. Subscribing through the plan page is your agreement to these software terms.

What you get. Both plans include a hosted careers page, candidate records, notes, basic search and offer letters. Starter includes one user and one active role. The paid plan includes up to five users, unlimited active roles, a custom careers-page design and additional AI tools. Current features, prices and usage limits are described on the plan page.

MS Rosie Starter. Starter automatically reads up to twenty-five resumes a month. Further resumes can still arrive and be read manually. General-interest applications do not use the active-role allowance. On a Starter account that has never been paid, a role closed for more than thirty days becomes read-only; its candidates and history are kept. Accounts that were previously paid do not have that closed-role restriction. Upgrading keeps your existing records. We give thirty days notice before changing Starter's plan limits.

Billing and renewal. The paid plan is billed in advance through Stripe, monthly or annually as selected at checkout. We give at least thirty days notice by email before a price change takes effect. You can update your payment method, see invoices and manage cancellation in Rosie's billing settings, or contact rosie@apply.ms. Normal subscription cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid month or year. There are no refunds for an unused part of a billing period. The annual plan includes a setup call by phone or video. Annual renewal is billed on the anniversary at the price then in effect.

An account included with recruiting. The partnership agreement governs the recruiting service and its charges. When that service ends, your Rosie records remain yours. Your account can continue on Starter, or you can choose the paid software plan. Ending recruiting does not require buying software to keep ownership of your records.

Closing an account or returning to Starter. In Rosie, closing a paid account means moving it to Starter, not deleting its records. The primary contact keeps sign-in access, the careers page stays published in the standard design, and paid features and additional seats are paused. The custom design and records are retained. An immediate account-closure request is different from cancelling renewal at the end of a paid period. Read the confirmation before choosing. You can return to the paid plan later. If your account needs a support-assisted change, contact us.

If a payment fails. We retry payment and notify you. An unresolved subscription eventually moves to Starter; it does not transfer ownership of your records or delete them. The primary contact keeps access to Starter, and the careers page stays published in its standard design. Paid features and additional seats are paused. See payment help for the current retry and downgrade process.

Your records and downloads. Your company's hiring records, the information you add and applications received through your careers page remain yours on every plan. You do not have to buy or renew a paid plan to request a copy of your records. Use the downloads available in Settings, or contact rosie@apply.ms if you need help obtaining your copy. Downloads respect account permissions and exclude Metcalf Search's private internal notes and raw interview transcripts, which remain locked under Rosie's transcript policy. Candidate write-ups and your own hiring notes are included where you have permission to access them. We do not sell your records or applicants' data.

Keeping or deleting records. We keep your account's records unless you request deletion. Inactivity, cancellation, a failed payment or a move to Starter is not a request to delete them. To request deletion or removal of your public careers page, write to rosie@apply.ms. We confirm the account and the scope of the request before acting. Closing a paid account in Rosie does not perform permanent deletion. Personal-information requests are handled under our privacy policy.

Applicants. You are the employer of record for every role on your careers page. You are responsible for how you treat applicants and for complying with the employment laws that apply to you. MS Rosie sends applicants nothing on your behalf except the receipt for their own application; every other message to a candidate is written and sent by you.

Referral credit. When a customer you invite becomes a paying subscriber, your next invoice is reduced by the percentage shown on your Settings screen for as long as that account keeps paying. The credit lapses the month the referred account stops paying. It applies to your subscription only and is never paid out in cash.

Acceptable use. Use MS Rosie for your own hiring. Do not post roles that do not exist, collect applications for a business you do not represent, or use MS Rosie to send unsolicited messages. We may suspend an account that is used that way, with notice by email where we can give it.

Help. Write to rosie@apply.ms, or read our public MS Rosie Help. We answer the same day on weekdays.
