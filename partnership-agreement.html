Partnership agreement

The agreement behind every Recruiting Partner, Plus and Pro subscription. Activating a tier accepts it.

This Agreement covers what each tier includes, onsite time, billing, changing tiers, cancellation with ten days notice, confidentiality, and the general terms of a Metcalf Search fractional recruiting partnership. Questions before you activate: book the thirty-minute fit call.

This Agreement ("Agreement") is made between Metcalf Search, LLC, an Ohio limited liability company ("Metcalf"), and the entity or individual subscribing to Metcalf's services ("Client"). The "Effective Date" of this Agreement is the date of the Client's initial subscription payment.

Partnership commitment: this Agreement engages Metcalf as a strategic recruiting and retention partner. It reflects our shared commitment to ethical, effective, and human-centered recruiting that strives to solve the "who" for your operation.

1. Partnership tiers and scope of service

1.1. Core services. All Partnership Tiers include Metcalf's complete, end-to-end recruiting service: strategic planning (job description and scorecard development); full-cycle candidate sourcing, screening and pipeline management; interview coordination and hiring manager calibration; offer management, negotiation and closing support; professional management of all inbound applications; a retention toolkit to help keep new and existing employees; and the MS Rosie hiring desk (careers page and applicant tracking) with job advertising spend.

1.2. Partnership service levels.

  • (a) Recruiting Partner. A strategic remote partnership. Includes a bi-weekly talent huddle (video), standard business day support by email and phone, a kickoff visit, and onsite support as needed at Metcalf's discretion. Customized retention toolkit. One (1) active role at a time.
  • (b) Recruiting Partner Plus. An embedded partnership. Includes a weekly talent huddle (video), standard business day support, and up to eight (8) hours of onsite time per month, scheduled with Client (for example, one full day or two half days). Enhanced, customized retention toolkit. Up to three (3) concurrent active roles.
  • (c) Recruiting Partner Pro. A fully integrated partnership. Includes a dedicated Metcalf recruiter onsite three (3) full days per week on a set schedule, priority support, and a weekly talent huddle. Enterprise, customized retention toolkit. All open roles within the scope agreed at kickoff.
  • (d) Onsite time. Onsite time may be used for interview days, hiring manager sessions, debriefs, and strategy sessions with leadership. Unused onsite time does not roll over to a later month and has no cash value.
  • (e) Additional onsite days. Client may request onsite time beyond what the selected tier includes. Each additional day is billed at a flat $2,500, including standard travel within the continental United States.
  • (f) Expedited travel. Visits scheduled less than 14 days out, or international travel, may incur additional costs with Client approval. All visits are subject to availability.

1.3. Service fees. The monthly fee for the selected tier is defined in Client's active subscription order at checkout. By keeping an active subscription, Client locks in the rate in effect at the time of initial payment ("Grandfathered Rate").

2. Term and billing

2.1. Term. This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues month to month ("Term").

2.2. Cancellation. Client may end this Agreement for any reason with ten (10) days written notice. Termination is effective at the end of the monthly billing cycle in which the notice period is satisfied.

2.3. Billing. The first invoice, for one full monthly fee, is due on activation. It sets the "Billing Anniversary" and covers the first month of service. All later monthly fees are billed automatically on the Billing Anniversary each month.

2.4. Payment method. Client agrees to provide and maintain a valid, automated payment method (ACH transfer or card on file) for all recurring monthly fees.

2.5. Late payment. If any payment is more than seven (7) days past due, Metcalf may pause services until payment is received, and Client's payment obligations continue.

3. Partnership and tier management

3.1. Changing tiers. Client may change tiers at any time through the Metcalf Search subscription portal. (a) Upgrades take effect immediately, with a one-time prorated charge for the difference through the end of the current billing cycle; the new monthly fee applies from the next Billing Anniversary. (b) Downgrades take effect immediately, with a prorated credit for the unused part of the higher tier applied to future invoices. No cash refunds are issued for mid-cycle downgrades.

3.2. Continuous partnership guarantee. This model replaces traditional per-hire guarantees. If a candidate placed by Metcalf resigns or is terminated for performance while this Agreement is active, Metcalf reopens the search for that role as part of the ongoing service. The guarantee applies only to candidates placed during the active term and for roles supported under this Agreement.

4. The Talent Points program

Talent Points are a voluntary loyalty incentive and do not create a monetary or contractual obligation.

4.1. Earning points. Client earns one (1) Talent Point for every ten dollars ($10.00) of paid monthly fee. Metcalf may also award discretionary bonus points for promotions, referrals, or other milestones.

4.2. Redemption and value. One (1) Talent Point is redeemable for one dollar ($1.00) of spending power on approved recruiting expenses, which may include sponsored job posts, premium candidate assessments, background checks, and candidate signing bonuses. Other search-related expenses can be approved in writing.

4.3. No cash value. Talent Points have no cash value, are not transferable, and cannot be redeemed for cash or as a discount on fees. All accumulated Talent Points are forfeited when this Agreement ends for any reason.

5. Confidentiality and conflicts of interest

5.1. Confidentiality. Metcalf treats all non-public information relating to Client (including hiring plans, salary bands, and candidate data) as strictly confidential, to the standard of a trusted fiduciary. Confidentiality obligations survive termination of this Agreement for two (2) years.

5.2. Conflicts of interest. Metcalf runs dedicated, separate searches for each client and never shares candidates between clients, except where a candidate applies independently or is otherwise publicly available. Metcalf will be fully transparent and seek Client's written consent if any potential conflict arises.

6. General terms

6.1. Fee disputes. Client agrees to resolve any fee dispute directly with Metcalf in good faith and not to initiate card chargebacks or bank payment disputes; doing so is a material breach of this Agreement.

6.2. Governing law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Ohio. The parties agree to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Franklin County, Ohio, for any dispute arising from this Agreement.

6.3. Scope of services. Metcalf acts as a strategic recruiting partner and advisor, not as legal counsel, an HR consultant, or a benefits administrator. All services, tools, and advice (including on compliance and retention) are best-practice frameworks from a talent acquisition perspective. Client is solely responsible for all final employment decisions, HR policies, and its own legal and regulatory compliance.

6.4. Effect of termination. (a) Grandfathered pricing holds only while the subscription stays continuously active; any cancellation, lapse, or non-payment ends it. (b) Reactivation after cancellation or lapse requires a new subscription at then-current pricing.

6.5. Limitation of liability. Except for gross negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of confidentiality, Metcalf's total liability under this Agreement will not exceed one (1) month of Client's current partnership fee.

6.6. Notices. Written notice under this Agreement may be given by email to info@metcalfsearch.com and to Client's email address on file.

6.7. Access to client systems. If Client gives Metcalf access to internal software, email, or data systems, that access is governed by a separate Data Security Addendum, which, once signed by both parties, is incorporated into this Agreement.

7. Acceptance and subscription

7.1. Acceptance. Client accepts this Agreement by making the initial subscription payment through the Metcalf Search payment portal.

7.2. Tier selection. Client's tier and monthly fee are confirmed by the subscription selected at checkout.

7.3. Binding effect. By keeping an active subscription, Client agrees to be bound by this Agreement, including its cancellation and pricing continuity terms.
