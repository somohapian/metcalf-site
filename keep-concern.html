Keep · Conversation guide

Work through an employee concern together.

Understand what happened, what may be getting in the way, and what each person can do about it.

Understand the concern before deciding what to do

Start with the expected work and a specific example of what happened. Separate what you observed from what someone reported or what you think it might mean.

Hear the employee’s account and the manager’s perspective. Check whether expectations were clear and whether priorities, tools, training, resources, or manager support need attention.

Agree on what will change, who owns each action, when you will review it, and what improvement you will look for. An action can be finished while the concern remains open. Review the effect before calling it resolved.
